Privacy Policy – AC Error Code Master

Version: 1.0  •  Updated: September 2026

AC Error Code Master is an offline HVAC technician reference app. This version does not require an account or sign-in and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

The app may store the selected language locally on the user's device. WhatsApp, email, Share and Report Problem actions open external apps or services; their own privacy policies apply after the user chooses to use them.

If advertising, analytics, cloud services, accounts, or other data-collecting features are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before that release.
